KEEPING BWAise CLEAN

Proper Waste Collection Makes a Difference

Poor waste disposal can contribute to environmental pollution, unpleasant surroundings and blocked drainage channels. Responsible collection and disposal help protect the community.

Bwaise Clean & Safe provides a platform for residents to access waste collection information and express their need for collection services.

MANAGE WASTE RESPONSIBLY

Good Waste Management Practices

Residents can contribute to a cleaner environment through simple daily practices.

🗑️

Use Proper Waste Containers

Keep rubbish in appropriate containers instead of leaving it in open spaces.

♻️

Separate Recyclable Materials

Where facilities are available, separate recyclable materials from general waste.

🚯

Avoid Illegal Dumping

Do not dump rubbish in drainage channels, roadsides, wetlands or other unauthorised areas.
