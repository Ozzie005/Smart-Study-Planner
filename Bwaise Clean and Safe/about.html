WHO WE ARE

Connecting People for Environmental Improvement

Bwaise Clean & Safe is a proposed community-focused web platform designed to support better waste management and environmental problem reporting in Bwaise, Kawempe Division, Kampala, Uganda.

The platform provides residents with a convenient way to report environmental concerns, access information about waste collection services, follow available report updates and learn practical environmental management practices.

It also aims to improve communication between residents, waste collection providers, local authorities and community organisations.

OUR DIRECTION

Our Mission and Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To provide an accessible digital platform that connects the Bwaise community with waste collection information, environmental reporting support and opportunities for community participation.

🌍

Our Vision

To contribute towards a cleaner, healthier and safer Bwaise through improved environmental awareness, responsible waste management and stronger community collaboration.

WHAT WE AIM TO ACHIEVE

Our Objectives

Our platform focuses on practical ways of supporting environmental improvement.

01

Improve Problem Reporting

Provide a convenient channel for residents to report illegal dumping, blocked drainage and flooding.

02

Support Waste Collection

Make information about waste collection providers and available services easier to access.

03

Encourage Accountability

Support report monitoring and communication about the progress of submitted complaints.

04

Promote Environmental Education

Share useful information about responsible waste disposal and drainage protection.

05

Strengthen Collaboration

Encourage cooperation between residents, service providers, authorities and community organisations.

06

Encourage Participation

Promote community activities and shared responsibility for environmental improvement.
